University’s additional holidays for students.

Chinese New Year
Monday, 16 February to Saturday 21 February 2026

There may be no lectures, tutorials, lab session, etc. Students are requested to view their course schedule for actual holiday/semester break dates during the Chinese New Year Holidays.

Hari Raya Puasa
Friday, 20 March to Tuesday 24 March 2026

There may be no lectures, tutorials, lab session, etc. Students are requested to view their course schedule for actual holiday/semester break dates during the Hari Raya. 

Deepavali
Saturday 7 November 2026

There may be no lectures, tutorials, lab session, etc.

Year End Break #
Monday, 21 December 2026 to Saturday, 2 January 2027

There may be no lectures, tutorials, lab session, etc.

# While there will be no classes on these dates, the following support services will be operational from 9am to 5pm. Please observe the dress code at all times.

- Computer Labs & Library, Cafeteria services, Student Services & Administrative Services, Fee Payment Services, Photocopying & Binding Services, and Shuttle Services (reduced frequency, please refer to APSpace)

Note:

  1. When a public holiday falls on a Sunday, the following day becomes a public holiday.
     
  2. The administrative and student services functions maybe operational during the additional holidays for students except for the Sunday, public holiday and certain approved days as indicated.
