# Demo script: 10-minute video

**Setup:** run `make reset-demo` before recording. It takes under 10 seconds. Open two windows: the dashboard and the Gemini Enterprise chat (or the ADK web UI).
The demo clock is fixed at **Thu 12 Nov 2026, 09:00 MYT** (`DEMO_NOW`), and the data is the committed snapshot. The demo needs no network access to APU.
**Demo lecturer:** `MZD` ("Assoc. Prof. Lecturer MZD", pseudonymised). On the committed snapshot, MZD's Friday 13 Nov gives **2 substitutes + 1 reschedule**, which is checked in `tests/test_substitutes.py::test_demo_lecturer_gets_substitutes_and_reschedules`.

| Time | Segment | What to show and say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | **Problem** | Overview page. *"APU's public timetable feed has 25,002 intake-session rows. That's 7,656 physical sessions across 1,143 intakes, 494 lecturers and 235 rooms, and about 600 sessions on a busy weekday."* Interview quote: `TBD-interview` ("When a lecturer calls in sick, we…"). *"APSpace shows timetables. Nothing re-plans them."* |
| 1:00–1:50 | **Architecture** | The diagram in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`. Real data (the pseudonymised public feed), real rules (handbook, holiday page and shuttle PDF quoted in `campus_ops_policy.md`), real engineering (OR-Tools CP-SAT, an MCP server on Cloud Run), and a human approving every step. |
| 1:50–2:00 | **Sign-in** | The persona screen: three roles, each with its own menu. *"Students can only read; lecturers fix their own classes; campus-wide changes need the timetabling office. The API enforces this."* |
| 2:00–4:15 | **Replan, small** | Sign in as **Lecturer MZD**. On the assistant home (the composer is lit from below), click *"I'm sick tomorrow, sort out my classes"*. The impact card appears, then the plan card: a segmented bar with 2 substitutes and 1 reschedule. Click **Prefer afternoon** to get v2, then **Approve & apply**. The executed card shows 3 events, 14 Gmail drafts and the change log. The rest of this segment: In chat: *"I'm MZD. I'm sick tomorrow, sort out my classes."* The agent replies: *"This affects 3 sessions, 12 intake groups and ~513 students."* Proposal: **2 sessions get a substitute.** KCC teaches the same module (Motion Graphics), is free and stays within 3 sessions that day, so the time and room stay the same and no student is affected. **1 session is rescheduled** (Web Design & Development, Fri 13 Nov 14:30 → Mon 16 Nov 10:45, D-07-15), with R-CLASH, R-NOTICE and R-SHUTTLE cited. Edit: *"Move the Web Design one to the afternoon"*. v2 lands on Fri 20 Nov 15:45. Then *"Approve"*. Show the Calendar events, the **Gmail drafts** (KCC is asked to confirm; the groups get a notice) and the Change Log Doc. On the dashboard Proposal page: EXECUTED, with links. |
| 4:15–6:00 | **Replan, the wow moment** | Sign in as **Timetabling Office**, say *"Monday 16 Nov is now a public holiday"*, then open **Campus** and pick Mon 16 Nov: the whole campus pulses red (145 rooms affected at 10:00). Switch to Tue 17 Nov, where the proposed moves glow green. Optionally show the Live board grid too. The original segment for reference: *"The government just declared Monday 16 Nov a public holiday."* Dashboard: Overview → "Monday 16 Nov is now a public holiday" → **598 sessions turn red** on the week grid → the solver runs (about 2.5 s, OPTIMAL) → the dots turn **green** across Tue–Fri. The amber dots are online fallbacks. Summary by school (faculty). The zero-clash check is PASSED. *"Evening classes can't stay physical: the last regular shuttle leaves at 19:15, so make-up classes must end by 19:00."* The agent replies with a summary by faculty and day, not 600 rows. |
| 6:00–7:30 | **Slot Finder** | In chat (or the dashboard preset): *"Schedule 6 FYP vivas of 45 minutes next week with examiners CMM, MSB and RJR in a presentation room."* It returns ranked slots (for example Tue 17 Nov 08:30–13:00 in Presentation Room 1 @ Cyber range), each with a reason. Pick #1, approve, and show the invites plus the Gmail draft. |
| 7:30–8:30 | **Insights** | 3D campus **Heat · week** mode (tall, bright rooms are the busiest) and the insights card (peak time, under-used rooms). Then: **Rooms:** the room × hour heatmap, utilisation per block, and empty rooms right now. **Timetable Health:** days over 6 h, 4+ sessions in a row, lecturer load, and the online vs physical mix. **Attendance Risk:** STU-DEMO-01 (synthetic) is at 88% with 23 sessions left. *"You can miss at most 2 more classes"* in Introduction To Business, calculated per module from real remaining sessions. In chat: *"How many more classes can STU-DEMO-01 miss?"* |
| 8:30–10:00 | **Impact and scale** | Measured metrics (`docs/METRICS.md`): holiday 598 sessions in about 2.5 s, 94% kept physical, 0 clashes; sick lecturer: 67% handled by a substitute with no student affected. Privacy: a public feed, pseudonymised staff, synthetic students, drafts only, and approval in the audit log. Roadmap: APSpace integration with APU's permission, other universities, and enterprise shift and room planning. |

## Backup plan
If the agent platform is unavailable during recording, drive the same flows from the dashboard. Its Overview "Try a disruption" buttons make the same MCP-equivalent calls. You can also use `make inspector` to show the raw MCP tools.
